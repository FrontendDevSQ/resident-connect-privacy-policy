Privacy Policy

Resident Connect · Effective 15 November 2026

Resident Connect is a community platform for residential societies. Residents can order from local vendors, post house and plot listings, submit complaints, read announcements and ask questions to a chatbot. Vendors can run a storefront. Society administrators manage the platform on the web.

This policy explains what information the Resident Connect mobile app and website collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to the Resident Connect Android app (package com.residentconnect) and the Resident Connect website at resident-connect.onrender.com.

1. Information we collect

We only collect information that you give us while using the app.

WhenWhat we collect
Resident registration and login Full name, email address and password.
Vendor registration and login Store or owner name, email address and password, or your Google account name and email if you choose Continue with Google.
Placing an order Delivery address, contact phone number and the items you order. Payment is cash on delivery only, so we do not collect card or bank details.
Posting a house or plot listing Title, property type, sale or rent, price, size, location, contact phone number, description and photos you upload.
Submitting a complaint Complaint category (water, electricity, security, maintenance or other) and the description you write.
Vendor storefront Store details, product information and product photos you upload.
Using the chatbot The questions you type, and any order details you give the chatbot when asking it to place an order.
Using the app Basic activity such as products you view, rate or add to your cart, which we use to show relevant products.

We do not collect your precise device location, contacts, call logs or messages. Any location you enter in a listing is text that you type yourself.

2. How we use your information

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To process and deliver your orders and let vendors fulfil them.
  • To show your listings to other residents and let interested people contact you.
  • To route your complaints to the management team and update you on their status.
  • To show announcements from the society administration.
  • To answer your questions through the chatbot.
  • To recommend products and improve the app.
  • To keep the platform safe, including reviewing vendor storefronts before they go live.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show third-party advertising in the app.

3. Who can see your information

  • Vendors see the name, delivery address and phone number on orders placed with them, so they can deliver.
  • Other residents see the details and photos you publish in a house or plot listing, including the contact number you add to it.
  • Society administrators and the management team can see accounts, complaints, orders and vendor information in order to run the platform.
  • Other users can see product ratings you submit.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

4. Service providers

We use these providers to run the platform. They process data on our behalf.

  • Render hosts our server and the files you upload.
  • MongoDB Atlas hosts our database.
  • Google Sign-In lets vendors sign in with a Google account. Google shares your name and email with us when you do.
  • Groq generates chatbot replies (see below).

5. Chatbot

The chatbot answers residents' questions about society procedures (SOPs), the marketplace and the platform. On a vendor's page it can also tell you which products that vendor has, what is available, and help you place an order.

When you use the chatbot, the text of your message, together with the relevant society information, vendor and product details needed to answer it, is sent to the Groq API to generate a reply. If you ask the chatbot to place an order, it asks you for your location, delivery address and contact number. These details are used only to place that order, and the order itself is then stored in the same way as any order placed in the app. Apart from what an order needs, please do not type passwords or other sensitive details into the chatbot.

6. Storage and security

Your data is stored on our backend server and database. Data travels between the app and our server over an encrypted HTTPS connection, and passwords are stored in hashed form, not as plain text. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and the personal data linked to it at any time by emailing residentconnect.support@gmail.com from the email address registered on the account. We will act on the request within 30 days. Some records, such as completed orders, may be kept in anonymised form where needed for the society's records or to meet legal obligations.

8. Your choices

  • You can view and update your profile details in the app.
  • You can delete your own listings and product photos.
  • You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction or deletion by emailing us.

9. Children

Resident Connect is intended for adult residents and vendors. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
