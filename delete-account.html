Delete Your Account

Resident Connect · Effective 18 November 2026

This page explains how residents and vendors of the Resident Connect app can request deletion of their account and the data linked to it.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to residentconnect.support@gmail.com from the email address you used to register.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my Resident Connect account.
  3. In the email, write your registered name and whether your account is a Resident or a Vendor account.
  4. We will confirm that the request comes from the account owner and then delete the account within 30 days. We will reply to you once it is done.

Request only some of your data? You can ask us to delete specific data without deleting your account, such as your listings, photos or a complaint. Email us at the address above and tell us what to remove.

Data that is deleted

  • Your account details: name, email address and password.
  • Your contact number and delivery addresses.
  • Your house or plot listings and the photos you uploaded.
  • Your complaints and any other content you submitted.
  • For vendors: your storefront, products and product photos.

Data that may be kept

  • Records of completed orders may be kept in anonymised form, where needed for the society's records or to meet legal obligations. These records are no longer linked to your name or contact details.

For full details of what we collect and how we use it, read our Privacy Policy.
